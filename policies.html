Shipping policy

We currently deliver within Chennai only. Enter your pincode on the store to see whether we deliver to you, the delivery charge and the usual delivery time. Orders above the free-delivery amount shown on the site are delivered free.

Every order is confirmed by us on WhatsApp before dispatch. A preferred delivery date is a request — we will confirm whether it is possible. Chocolates are heat-sensitive; please refrigerate or keep them in a cool place on arrival.

Refund & cancellation policy

As our products are food items, we cannot accept returns once delivered. If your order arrives damaged, incorrect or incomplete, please WhatsApp us within 24 hours of delivery with photos and your order number, and we will replace the item or refund the amount for it.

You can cancel an order free of charge before we confirm it. Once an order is confirmed and being prepared, cancellation may not be possible. Approved refunds are made to the original payment method.

Privacy policy

We collect the details you give us — name, mobile number, email and delivery address — only to process and deliver your order, respond to enquiries and, if you subscribe, send our newsletter. We do not sell your information. Your details are stored securely with our database provider and are visible only to Romancham staff who handle orders.

You can ask us to update or delete your details at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp. To stop the newsletter, reply to any newsletter email or message us.

Terms & conditions

Prices on the site are in Indian Rupees and include applicable taxes. Product photos are representative; handmade items may vary slightly. All orders are subject to availability and our confirmation. Placing an order on the site is a request — the order is accepted only when we confirm it and payment is received.

These terms are governed by the laws of India, with courts in Chennai having jurisdiction.
